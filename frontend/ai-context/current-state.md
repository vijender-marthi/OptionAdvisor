# Current Product Decisions

- Reduce confusion around Discovery/Scanner.
- Trade Command Center will aggregate Day, Swing, and Regular recommendations.
- Watchlist is for potential pre‑entry opportunities.
- Positions Center captures entered trades; it will evolve from the existing portfolio page.
- Alert Center shows all alerts.
- Regular Portfolio page exists and should be refactored into the Positions Center.
- Desired workflow: Trade Command Center → Watchlist → Positions Center → Alert Center.
