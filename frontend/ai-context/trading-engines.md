# Trading Engines

## Day Trade Engine
- Same‑day trades, primarily options.
- Uses VWAP, opening‑range breakout, volume spike, and QQQ/SPY direction.
- Avoids overnight holding unless explicitly converted to a swing trade.

## Swing Trade Engine
- 2–5 day trades on stocks and options.
- Looks for trend continuation, breakout quality, relative strength, and late‑day confirmation.

## Regular Trade Engine
- 2–4 week horizon.
- Supports options spreads, long calls/puts, and stock positions.
- Considers IV rank, probability, expiry, support/resistance, and sector strength.

### Trade Classification
- **Day** – fast momentum.
- **Swing** – trend capture.
- **Regular** – strategic, longer‑term trades.
